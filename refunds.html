1. Physical goods (tea and teaware, paid via Stripe)

Right to cancel. Under the Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013 you may cancel your order within 14 days of the day you receive the goods, without giving a reason. Tell us by emailing hello@teafairyworld.co.uk with your order number, then return the goods within 14 days of telling us. We will refund the price and standard delivery cost within 14 days of receiving the goods back (or proof of return).

Exceptions. The right to cancel does not apply to goods that are liable to deteriorate or expire rapidly, or to sealed food and drink goods (including tea) that are unsealed or opened after delivery and are unsuitable for return for health protection or hygiene reasons. Tea must be returned unopened and in its original sealed packaging.

4. Digital courses (Apple In-App Purchase)

Courses in the TeaTok app are sold through Apple In-App Purchase, and Apple processes all payments and refunds for them. To request a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple Account and select the purchase. We cannot issue refunds for in-app purchases directly. To recover courses on a new device, use Profile → Settings → Restore purchases.
