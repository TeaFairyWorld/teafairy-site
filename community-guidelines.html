1. Scope

These guidelines apply to everything you post in TeaFairy World, including brew check-ins (photos, notes and comments about your tea sessions) and reviews, which only verified buyers of a product can write. They form part of our Terms of Use.

2. Be kind and genuine

  • Share your own photos, tasting notes and honest experiences.
  • Reviews must reflect your real experience of a product you bought. No paid, incentivised or fake reviews.
  • Respect other tea lovers of every background and level of experience.

3. Zero tolerance

We have zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users. You must not post:

  • Sexual, violent, hateful, discriminatory or otherwise objectionable content.
  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or sharing others’ personal information.
  • Spam, scams, unsolicited advertising, links to external shops, or misleading content.
  • Offers of illegal, counterfeit or regulated goods (e.g. drugs, weapons), or content encouraging illegal or dangerous activity, including unsafe health claims.
  • Content that infringes others’ copyright, trademarks or privacy.

4. Reporting and blocking

Tap the “…” menu on any check-in, review, comment or profile and choose Report or Block. Blocking hides that user’s content from you immediately. We review reports within 24 hours. You can also email hello@teafairyworld.co.uk.

5. Enforcement

We may remove content and warn, suspend or permanently ban users who break these guidelines, acting within 24 hours of a report for objectionable content. Serious or illegal content may be reported to the relevant authorities. If you think we made a mistake, email us to appeal. [TODO: confirm appeals process and moderation tooling]

6. Changes

We may update these guidelines; we will notify you of material changes in the app.
