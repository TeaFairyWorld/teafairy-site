Meet Tea Fairy Bao Lili

Bao Lili (鲍丽丽), known as “Tea Fairy” (茶仙子), is a China Tea Culture Promotion Ambassador with 26 years of tea practice. She has visited 139 tea mountains, worked with 68 intangible cultural heritage inheritors, and presented Chinese tea at four World Expos. She is TeaFairy World’s lead tea-brewing instructor.
