Expo 2025 Osaka: 醒悟 Tea Awakening receives an Expo Gold Award

醒悟普洱茶荣获2025年大阪世博会金奖

1 November 2025

At the close of Expo 2025 Osaka (13 October 2025, in Osaka, Japan), the ancient-tree Pu’er tea “醒悟” (Tea Awakening), created by TeaFairy (茶仙子), China Tea Culture Promotion Ambassador and a presence at four World Expos, received an Expo Gold Award from the World Expo Commissioners Club (世博会馆长联合会). It marks further high recognition for Chinese tea culture on the international stage.

2025年大阪世博会闭幕之际（10月13日在日本大阪），中国茶文化推广大使、四届世博会 TeaFairy（茶仙子）创立的“醒悟”古树普洱茶，荣获世博会馆长联合会颁发的世博金奖，标志着中国茶文化在国际舞台上再获高度认可。

Expo 2025 Osaka lasted 184 days and drew 25 million visitors from around the world. The Expo is a great stage on which human civilisations gather. This edition brought together leading displays from many countries. The China Pavilion was organised around three themes — Chinese civilisation, scientific and technological innovation, and future development — and the “醒悟茶席” (Tea Awakening tea setting) was a highlight of the traditional-culture exhibition hall.

大阪世博会历时184天，吸引全球2500万参观者。世博会是人类文明汇聚的大舞台，这场盛会汇聚各国顶尖展示，中国馆以中华文明、科技创新及未来发展为三大主题，其中“醒悟茶席”成为传统文化展厅的亮点。

This award continues the long association between Chinese tea and the Expo. At the 1915 Panama-Pacific International Exposition, Chinese tea first won gold. A century later, “醒悟” (Tea Awakening) has brought Chinese tea back onto the Expo podium. “The last time Chinese tea received this award was more than a hundred years ago,” TeaFairy (茶仙子) said with feeling. “That ‘醒悟’ can receive an Expo Gold Award is a great recognition of the outstanding quality of Chinese tea.”

此次获奖延续中国茶与世博的深厚渊源。1915年巴拿马万国博览会上，中国茶首获金奖。百年后，“醒悟”代表中国茶再登世博领奖台。“上一次中国茶拿到这个奖还是在一百多年前，”TeaFairy（茶仙子）动情地说，“‘醒悟’能获得世博金奖，是对中国茶优异品质的极大认可。”

“醒悟” (Tea Awakening) ancient-tree Pu’er is a tea created by TeaFairy (茶仙子). Its inspiration came from visiting ancient tea trees in a hundred remote mountains of Yunnan, China. TeaFairy (茶仙子) has spoken of the original impulse behind “醒悟”: “It began in a primary forest, beneath a 3,200-year-old ancient tea tree. The wind brought a clear, sweet fragrance, and two characters appeared in my mind: ‘醒悟’.”

“醒悟”古树普洱茶是 TeaFairy（茶仙子）的作品茶，其灵感来源于走访中国云南百座深山古茶树的经历。TeaFairy（茶仙子）分享了创立“醒悟”的初心：“初心就是我在原始森林，在那棵3200年的古茶树下，那个风送过来清甜的香气，脑海里浮现了两个字‘醒悟’。”

“醒悟” is made from ancient-tree tea more than 600 years old, from four mountains: Banzhang, Bingdao, Yiwu and Jingmai. It follows a traditional craft of spring picking, summer sun-drying, autumn pressing and winter storage; it is given the full sun of eight days and is stone-milled and hand-pressed into 150-gram cakes. It is known for a sun-honey aroma, a long flavour that is both firm and soft, sweet and lively, and a quality that grows more fragrant with age. Storage is equally exacting: the tea is kept in a professional tea warehouse that is dust-free, odour-free and protected from light, at 10–25°C and 55–70% humidity, with a professional team recording temperature and humidity every day to safeguard the tea as it transforms.

“醒悟”选用班章、冰岛、易武、景迈四大山头600年以上树龄的古树茶为原料，经春采、夏晒、秋压、冬藏的传统工艺，经历8个太阳的充足日晒，以石磨手工压制成150克茶饼。具有太阳蜜香、刚柔甜活的绵长滋味和越陈越香的品质特征。其仓储环节同样讲究：茶叶存储在无尘、无异味、避光，温度10-25°C、湿度55%-70%的专业茶仓，由专业团队每日记录温湿度，确保转化品质。

TeaFairy (茶仙子) stresses: “Tea is a living thing. Only professional, high-quality storage can guarantee the quality and safety of Pu’er tea.”

TeaFairy（茶仙子）强调：“茶是有生命的。专业高品质的仓储才能保证普洱茶的品质和安全。”

The honours earned by “醒悟” (Tea Awakening) Pu’er tea are an affirmation, over many years, of reverence for nature and fidelity to tradition. Since it was created in 2010, it has repeatedly been called on for cultural exchange: as a state gift at the 2014 APEC Economic Leaders’ Meeting in China; as the tea served at the 2019 China–France leaders’ tea gathering at Yu Garden during the second China International Import Expo; at the 2020 art exhibition marking the 30th anniversary of the development and opening of Pudong; at commemorative events for the 100th anniversary of the founding of the Communist Party of China in 2021; and as a representative of Chinese tea culture at Expo 2025 Osaka — an Expo Gold Award tea.

“醒悟”普洱茶醒悟的荣誉是多年来对敬畏自然、坚守传统的肯定。自2010年诞生以来，屡次承担文化交流使命：2014年中国APEC首脑峰会大国之礼、2019年第二届进博会中法领导人豫园茶叙专用茶、2020年浦东开发开放30周年艺术展、2021年中国共产党建党100周年纪念活动，以及2025年大阪世博会中国茶文化代表-世博金奖茶。

An Expo Gold Award tea is not only a benchmark of quality; it is also a bright calling card for Chinese culture, continuing to tell the world stories of Eastern wisdom. “不以香艳立世，只以醇厚传家” — not established in the world by showy fragrance, but passed down through mellow depth. “醒悟” (Tea Awakening) ancient-tree Pu’er holds value for drinking, collecting and culture. With its distinctive flavour and cultural depth, it invites tea lovers around the world to experience the breadth of Chinese tea culture, helps the world fall in love with Chinese tea, and builds a distinguished brand image for China’s high-end Pu’er.

世博金奖茶不仅是茶叶品质的标杆，更是一张张闪亮的中国文化名片，持续向世界讲述着东方的智慧与故事。“不以香艳立世，只以醇厚传家”，“醒悟”古树普洱茶兼具品饮、收藏与文化价值，正以其独特风味和文化底蕴，引领全球茶友领略中国茶文化的博大精深，让世界爱上中国茶，树立起中国高端普洱茶的卓越品牌形象。
