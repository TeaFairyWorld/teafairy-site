About TEAFAIRY LIMITED

TEAFAIRY LIMITED is a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17464103) and operates the Tea Fairy (茶仙子) brand and the TeaTok app.

Our founder

Baolili (鲍丽丽) is a China tea culture ambassador with 26 years of tea practice. She has presented Chinese tea at four World Expos — Shanghai, Milan, Dubai and Osaka — and has worked across 139 tea mountains with 68 intangible cultural heritage inheritors.

Our mission

To teach tea lovers at home and abroad to brew tea together and to fall in love with drinking tea. 收藏岁月，醒悟人生.
